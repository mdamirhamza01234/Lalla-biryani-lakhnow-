LALLA BIRYANI — WEBSITE TEMPLATE

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the folder contents to your hosting provider.
3. Internet is required for Google Fonts, the food photography and the embedded Google Map.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Responsive one-page restaurant website
- Our Story, Menu, Find Us sections
- Tap-to-call phone link and Google Maps directions
- Mobile navigation

IMPORTANT CONTENT NOTES
- Business name, address and phone are based on the Google Maps listing shared in the conversation.
- The listed menu dishes are indicative examples, not a verified live menu.
- Opening hours and prices should be confirmed with the restaurant before publishing.
- The “Since 1985” line is based on the listing/description previously discussed; confirm with the owner before using it as a formal claim.
- Replace the remote image URLs in style.css with licensed restaurant photographs for the strongest authentic result.
- This is a front-end website template; it does not include online ordering, payment, admin dashboard or hosting.
